Marriage biodata maker

Fill in the details, add a photo if you wish, and print one neat A4 page. No login, no watermark, and nothing leaves your device.

How to use the maker

  1. Type the details. Any box left empty is left out of the page, so skip what does not apply. The horoscope section appears only if you fill it.
  2. Add your own rows. Every section has "Add your own row" for anything particular to your family or tradition, with a label of your choice in any language.
  3. Top line. If your family begins a biodata with an auspicious line, type it in the first box. It is left out if empty.
  4. Photo (optional). Choose a recent, natural portrait. It is shrunk and kept in your browser only.
  5. Choose a style and colour, and watch the "fits on one page" line.
  6. Print or save as PDF, or copy the text for WhatsApp.

Getting a clean PDF

Press "Print or save as PDF". In the print window choose "Save as PDF" as the destination, A4 paper, margins "None" (or "Default"), switch off "Headers and footers" and tick "Background graphics" so that the border and colour print. On a phone, use Print in the browser menu and then "Save as PDF". The file is named with the person's name.

Two ways to share

"Copy as text" gives the whole biodata as plain text. "Copy without contact details" leaves out the contact section, which is the safer version for posting in a community group; interested families can then ask the group admin or the person who posted. Read sharing a biodata safely before you post anywhere.

Writing in Hindi or another language

You can type in any language your keyboard supports. Change the title to, for example, "बायोडाटा", and use "Add your own row" where you want a label in your own language. See the हिंदी फॉर्मेट.

What to write

The format guide explains each section. For the two parts in your own words, see the about me examples and partner preference wording; for the family section, family details.

Privacy

The text and photo are stored only in this browser on this device, so that they are here when you return. "Clear all" deletes them. On a shared or cyber-café computer, press "Clear all" before leaving. We never see what you type.

Questions people ask

Is the marriage biodata maker free?

Yes. No charge, no login and no watermark.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The photo is resized and stored only in your own browser.

Can I make the biodata in Hindi?

Yes. Type in Hindi in the boxes, change the title, and add your own rows with Hindi labels.

Can I edit it later?

Yes, in the same browser. Use "Download backup" to keep a copy or move it to another device.
